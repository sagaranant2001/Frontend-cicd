The Taj Hotel

Mumbai's landmark by the sea

The Taj Mahal Palace Hotel in Mumbai is one of India's most celebrated landmarks. Founded by the industrialist Jamsetji Tata, it opened its doors in December 1903, which makes it more than a century old. The hotel stands right beside the Gateway of India and faces the Arabian Sea, so guests enjoy some of the best harbour views in the city. Its Indo-Saracenic architecture blends Indian, Islamic and Victorian styles, and its famous dome, grand arches and stately corridors make it instantly recognisable. Walking through its lobby feels like stepping into a living piece of history. For many travellers, the Taj is a symbol of Mumbai itself.

Inside, the hotel is known for its elegant rooms and suites, award-winning restaurants and warm Taj hospitality. It is part of the Taj group, run by IHCL, and over the years it has welcomed royalty, world leaders, artists and film stars. In November 2008 the hotel was badly affected by the terror attacks on Mumbai. After careful restoration it reopened the same year, and it has since become a symbol of courage and resilience. Today the Taj remains a top choice for visitors who want history, comfort and a memorable stay by the sea.
